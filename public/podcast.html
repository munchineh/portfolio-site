Turning flashcards into a podcast

A Korean listening podcast built from my class syllabus. AI-voiced, localised into eleven languages, and played in a web app designed for shadowing.

Role
Product design, content design, AI voice design
Timeline
Oct 2026 to now
Tools
Claude, Gemini 3.8 TTS, ElevenLabs
Platform
Web app on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare R2
Listen to an episode
What came of itOngoing
89episodes across three levels, from 3,000+ of my flashcards
11subtitle languages, with the interface to match

Still in progress: up to nine episodes voiced a day, with all three levels projected to finish by the end of October 2026.

01

Building the app I couldn't find

Great Korean podcasts and shadowing apps are everywhere. None follow my Sogang syllabus.

Flashcards give one sentence at a time, and NotebookLM improvised at native speed with vocab outside the books. So I built my own.

02

Orchestrating multiple AI models

No single model could write, voice and check an episode well, or cheaply.

So each job went to the model that does it best, or costs the least.

ClaudeScripts, player, pipeline and daily pronunciation review
Gemini 3.8 TTSEpisode voices, free
ElevenLabsCard voices, sound effects and audio QA
Gemini in AntigravityBulk translation, saving Claude credits
Whisper + an alignerSyllable timing for the highlighting, free on my Mac
Voice matchCatches lines read in the wrong voice
$0to voice an episode on the free tier
10requests a day, run on a schedule from my Mac
03

UX designed for shadowing

Shadowing means hearing the same line over and over, and scrubbing back for it gets old fast.

Replay line

Repeat the current line in one tap.

Speed control

0.7× to 1.2×, from first listen to real pace.

Picks up where you left off

Each episode resumes where you stopped, no sign-in.

04

Responsive PWA design, from phone to widescreen

A study app gets opened on whatever screen is nearest: a phone on the subway, a foldable on the sofa, a monitor at a desk.

05

Localised into eleven languages

My classmates come from all over, and English subtitles only reach some of them.

Subtitles and interface switch together. It installs to the home screen and shares with a link.
