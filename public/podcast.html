Turning flashcards into a podcast

A Korean listening podcast built from my class syllabus. AI-voiced, localised into eleven languages, and played in a web app designed for shadowing.

Role
Product design, content design, AI voice design
Timeline
Oct 2026 to now
Built with
Claude, Gemini 3.8 TTS, ElevenLabs
Platform
Web app on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare R2
Listen to an episode
What came of itOngoing
89episodes across three levels, from 3,000+ of my flashcards
11subtitle languages, with the interface to match

Still in progress: up to nine episodes voiced a day, with all three levels projected to finish by the end of October 2026.

01

No app matched the syllabus

Great Korean podcasts and shadowing apps are everywhere. None follow my Sogang syllabus.

Flashcards give one sentence at a time, and NotebookLM improvised at native speed with vocab outside the books. So I built my own.

On syllabusOnly what's in the books

Every word and grammar point comes from the Level 2, 3 and 4 books.

Sounds realLike a drama, at a pace I can follow

Real situations, read slowly enough to repeat.

ShareableFor my classmates too

Subtitles in their languages. No install, no account.

02

AI voice design, tested on 4,000+ cards

I first voiced 4,000+ flashcards with ElevenLabs, and tuned them until they sounded human.

4B.3 읽고 말하기 1
Word / Phrase
조만간
Meaning
Before long, one of these days, soon
Example
조만간 밥 한번 먹자! 진짜로, 빈말 아니야.
Translation
Let's grab a meal soon! For real, I'm not just saying it.
Hanja

早晚間
早晚间 zǎowǎnjiān · Chinese says 不久 bùjiǔ

Notes

vs 곧: 곧 is soon with a clear near time (곧 도착해요); 조만간 is a vaguer "one of these days".

조만간 한번 보자 = let's meet up soon · 조만간 연락할게 = I'll be in touch soon

  1. Tap to hear it

    Real card audio, directed as "cheerful, casual".

  2. The word lights up in the example

    Used in a sentence people actually say.

  3. A thumb-sized play button

    A full-width bar in place of Anki's small icon.

  4. Hanja, linked to Chinese

    Original characters, plus simplified Chinese and pinyin.

  5. Notes that compare

    Look-alike words and common pairings.

  1. ProblemEvery line sounded like a listening test

    Polished, but lifeless.

    FixCast voices by register

    Younger voices for 반말, older ones for 합쇼체, each with an acting direction.

  2. ProblemSound changes came out wrong

    똑바로 was read as 똑까로.

    FixRespell what the voice reads

    The voice gets 똑빠로, the card keeps the normal spelling.

  3. ProblemDoubled words and directions read aloud

    Easy to miss by ear.

    FixAuto-check every clip with speech-to-text

    Clips that don't match their card re-record automatically.

Long-form audio would have cost about three months of ElevenLabs credits, so the podcast moved to Gemini's free tier.

03

Content design: textbook to real life

Textbook dialogues are tidy. Real conversations aren't.

Each card is written into a scene where people would really say it, with speech levels that fit the relationship.

OverlapsPeople talk over each other

Interruptions cut in early, like real talk.

NoiseNot every room is quiet

Restaurant chatter and other ambience.

FeelingExcited, tired, mumbling

Laughs, sighs and trailing-off words.

05

AI voice design: a voice for every character

A Gemini request holds two voices. My scenes needed grandparents, a taxi driver from Busan and a buyer from Tokyo.

So every character got a voice of their own, and kept it across all three levels.

  1. ProblemOlder characters sounded thirty

    Gemini's voices skew young.

    FixAge the voice first

    A small shift in formant, pitch and pace makes a voice older. ElevenLabs steps in only when that falls short.

  2. ProblemAccents disappeared

    ElevenLabs Multilingual v2 kept them, but the lines came out flat.

    FixDescribe the accent in the voice

    Gemini's designed voices take it from the prompt: Busan, Jeolla, Gyeongju, Tokyo, Hanoi, Melbourne.

  3. ProblemShort replies sounded read out alone

    A dry 그건 인정. came out flat every time.

    FixVoice the whole exchange

    Then keep only the reply, so it sounds like an answer.

Every new voice gets a test line before its episode is voiced.

06

QA for Korean pronunciation

Korean is written one way and said another. AI voices sometimes read the spelling.

Every language does this. In English, "handbag" sounds like "hambag".

Nasalisation
국물said as궁물

ㄱ softens to ㅇ before ㅁ.

Palatalisation
같이said as가치

ㅌ before 이 becomes ㅊ, like "gotcha".

Tensing
똑바로said as똑빠로

ㅂ hardens to ㅃ after ㄱ.

  1. Respell what the voice reads

    Rules rewrite each sentence as it's said before voicing. Learners still see the normal spelling.

  2. Find the gap in QA

    Speech-to-text checks spelling, not sound, so pronunciation needed its own check.

  3. Test three ways

    Same sentence, three spellings, two engines. All sounded alike, so the models mostly know the rules.

  4. Fix what's left

    Slips came across word gaps in long takes, like 고객 문의. Now risky phrases are joined, numbers are spelled out, and Claude reviews each script before voicing.

One shared pronunciation tool for cards and podcast, with zero re-voicing costs.
07

Review ops: one sheet, blockers first

By Level 3, my reviews lived across three separate pages.

I folded them into one sheet, sorted by the day each decision is due. Anything that stops an episode from voicing turns red and moves to the top.

FocusI review audio only

Claude settles the script notes. I judge what needs ears.

DecisionsThree answers per voice

Pass keeps the Gemini voice. Fail means I bring an ElevenLabs voice. Depends gives Gemini one more direction.

ChecksA voice print on every line

It flags any line read in the wrong voice. The first run caught 11 lines in 20 episodes.
