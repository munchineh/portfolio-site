Simplifying operational tools

A pre-construction SaaS for the German market, built from zero: quotes, task management and Gantt scheduling. Designed for consultants on tablets and contractors on phones.

Role
Founding designer, freelance
Timeline

Client
Buildlr

What came of itShipped solo

One designer, from discovery to launch.

01

One quote, two very different days

Buildlr takes a building job from the first client visit to the last task on site.

Every quote runs five tiers deep, from project down to sub-task. The challenge: keep that much detail manageable on a tablet in a client's living room and a phone on site, with one design system.

02

Only the tier you need

Progressive disclosure keeps a five-tier quote from turning into a wall of numbers.

A persistent tree keeps consultants oriented, tasks switch on as rows, and pricing recalculates live. One task opens at a time, so the page stays readable at forty rows deep.

03

The question lives in the form

Consultants kept stalling on details only the client could answer.

So requests for information (RFIs) live inside the task. A pre-written question with a deadline goes straight to the client's phone, and the line stays flagged until it's answered.

04

Same tree, one thumb

The mobile app reads the same quote, one contractor at a time.

Rooms became chips, categories became accordions and tasks became cards, so the desktop hierarchy carries over. Client blockers are flagged in red, and a running task collapses to the essentials.
